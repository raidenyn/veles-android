# Android plain-GATT sharing implementation plan

> **For agentic workers:** Use superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task.

**Goal:** Serve bounded synthetic records through a user-enabled Android BLE foreground service.

**Architecture:** A pure Kotlin transport owns framing, per-connection reassembly and delivery queues. An Android adapter owns GATT callbacks and advertising; the foreground service owns lifecycle and explicit availability. A small Home control starts sharing from a visible action.

**Tech Stack:** Kotlin, Android BluetoothGattServer, Compose, JUnit/MockK.

**Spec:** `docs/rfcs/2026-08-21-otp-external-devices.md`, OTP-06; `docs/protocol/veles-v1.md`, section 9.

## Global constraints
- Fixed service/write/notify UUIDs from OTP2-GAT-001; write-without-response and notify only; no bonding or encrypted characteristic permissions.
- MTU >=185; record length 1..4096; eight clients maximum.
- Four-byte fragment headers; exact MTU-7 non-final payload; transaction IDs 1..255.
- Five-second monotonic inter-fragment expiry; conflict escalation within 60 seconds.
- Queues: 16 messages/16 KiB per peer, 128 messages/128 KiB global. Drop newest on overflow.
- No production OTP integration, pairing, cryptography, or final Connections UX.

## Review focus
- Late callbacks after stop/restart must not resurrect advertising or sessions.
- Permission revocation and Bluetooth-off must close state without crashing.
- A stalled notification must not block another client's queue.
- Malformed fragments must release partial buffers without delivering records.
- Process restoration must distinguish enabled intent from actual availability.
